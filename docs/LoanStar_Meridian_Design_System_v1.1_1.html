LoanStar · Design System · v1.1

Meridian

A navigation-grade interface system for the LoanStar lending platform. Deep navy carries authority, teal signals action and approval, and every figure — amounts, IDs, dates — is set in a tabular mono ledger face.

Three faces, three jobs. Sora for headings and product identity. Public Sans for interface text and paragraphs. JetBrains Mono for every number that matters — loan amounts, reference IDs, rates, and dates in tables. Tabular figures keep ledgers aligned.

FAB, preferences & FAQ accordion
How is my monthly amortization computed?
Principal plus flat add-on interest for the full term, divided by the number of months. The exact breakdown appears on your disclosure statement before you sign.
Can I pay off my loan early?
Yes — request an early settlement quote from your account officer to see the adjusted payoff amount.
What happens if my vessel deployment is delayed?
Contact us before your first due date. Deferment options are available for documented deployment changes.
