# Loanstar final handover presentation design

## Purpose

Create a practical operational handover presentation for the Loanstar Seafarer Lending Platform. The meeting will seek final client acceptance after accepted UAT and will emphasize the live system walkthrough plus the secure transfer of access and operational ownership.

## Audience and delivery

The presentation is for the authorized client approver, client operational participants, Rovick Romasanta as StartupLab Project Owner, and Richard Prodigalidad as StartupLab CEO. It supports a Tuesday-to-Friday handover window and uses a practical operational walkthrough tone.

## Source facts

- Client: Loan Star Lending Group Corporation
- Project: Loanstar Seafarer Lending Platform
- UAT version: 1.0 (Meridian DS V1.1)
- UAT period: September 22 to 25, 2026
- UAT decision: Accepted on September 25, 2026
- UAT evidence: 109 documented test cases marked Passed
- Production URL: https://loanstar.vercel.app/
- UAT exclusions: AI Assistant Drawer backend endpoints are inactive; legal policy footer pages are placeholders; Remember Me uses standard browser cookie retention without custom persistent-token overrides

## Deck structure

Use a shortened, 12-slide adaptation of the supplied Final Project Handover template. Retain its title treatment, color palette, footer, section labels, typography, table style, and slide numbering. Remove unused template slides rather than introducing a new design.

1. Cover: project, client, handover date, production version, Project Owner.
2. Handover purpose and decision: confirm what transfers and record final acceptance.
3. Project completion summary: objective, final scope, UAT outcome, production URL, known limitations.
4. Final scope and UAT acceptance: summarize accepted business workflows and the 109 passed UAT cases.
5. Delivered business workflows: onboarding, underwriting, servicing and recovery, management and administration, system controls.
6. Live production walkthrough: production URL and the controlled walkthrough checks.
7. Access and ownership transfer: list production, hosting, domain, source repository, and third-party services. Mark recipient and status as confirmation items where evidence is absent.
8. Documents, training, and responsibilities: list the required handover items and operational owners, using confirmation labels where not supplied.
9. Support, warranty, and escalation: use the policy route while marking commercial terms and dates for confirmation.
10. Remaining actions and known limitations: record the three UAT exclusions without misrepresenting them as completed functionality.
11. Final handover decision and sign-off: Accepted as the intended decision, with formal written confirmation at the meeting.
12. Closure and post-handover review: actions immediately after acceptance, access cleanup, hypercare, and a 15-to-30-day review.

## Content safeguards

Only UAT-verified facts become completion claims. No passwords, tokens, private keys, or recovery codes will appear. Unconfirmed client administrators, ownership assignments, support hours, warranty dates, evidence locations, and handover date will display as "To be confirmed during handover." The deck will not claim that the production deployment, data transfer, training, or access transfer has already occurred unless the supplied records establish it.
